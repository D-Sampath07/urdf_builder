Point-based joint placement · experimental

Pick points directly on the selected joint's child mesh. Set A and B represent the two wheels/features. Their centres define the joint axis.

Picking is off.

For circle fit, pick 4–8 points on the same flat circular face for each wheel. For triangle-like features, choose balanced points around each feature and use Average. Derive axis sets the joint origin at the midpoint and aligns joint Z with A → B. You can still edit the origin or gizmo manually.
